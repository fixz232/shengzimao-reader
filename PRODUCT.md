# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who want a discreet, compact desktop reading tool while working at a Windows computer. They need fast access to reading controls without drawing attention or interrupting their main workflow.

## Product Purpose

The app provides a single-line reader that can disguise itself as ordinary window text, import local novels, keep reading progress, and expose compact controls for styling, navigation, bookmarks, and window behavior. Success means the reader stays stable, subtle, and quick to operate.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, practical, unobtrusive. The interface should feel like a small utility rather than a branded product or decorative reading app.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page composition, oversized hero styling, decorative cards, loud color themes, and controls that hide standard desktop affordances. The settings screen should not feel playful or ceremonial.

## Design Principles

Keep controls direct and familiar.
Prioritize stable window behavior over visual novelty.
Make stealth behavior configurable and reversible.
Preserve dense settings without making them cramped.
Treat color, opacity, and font changes as precise utility controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast by default, preserve keyboard navigation where practical, support reduced visual distraction, and avoid relying on color alone for state.
